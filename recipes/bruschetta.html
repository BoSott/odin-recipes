Bruschetta

Home

Description

Classic bruschetta is an Italian appetizer featuring diced fresh tomatoes and basil served over toasted crusty bread (typically baguette or ciabatta) that has been rubbed with garlic.

Ingredients

  • baguette or rustic bread
  • garlic
  • olive oil
  • Roma toatoes
  • Fresh basil
  • balsamic vinegar
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. To prepare the topping, dice Roma or plum tomatoes, remove excess seeds or liquid to prevent sogginess, and toss them with extra virgin olive oil, minced garlic, fresh basil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper. Let the mixture marinate for 10–30 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
  2. For the base, slice a baguette or rustic bread into thick pieces and toast them in an oven or on a grill until golden and crisp.
  3. While still warm, rub each slice with a cut garlic clove and drizzle with olive oil.
  4. Spoon the tomato mixture generously over the toasted bread just before serving to maintain crispness.
  5. Serve and enjoy happy guests
