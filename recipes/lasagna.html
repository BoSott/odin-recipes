Lasagna

Home

Description

A classic, hearty Italian dish featuring layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, and creamy cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound vegan beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated

Steps

  1. Boil lasagna noodles according to package directions and drain.
  2. Brown ground beef in a skillet, then mix with marinara sauce.
  3. In a bowl, combine ricotta cheese and half of the parmesan cheese.
  4. In a 9x13 inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce.
  5. Place a layer of noodles over the sauce, followed by ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.
  6. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, ending with cheese on top.
  7. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 45 minutes, then let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
