Description

Tiramisu is a classic Italian coffee-flavored dessert consisting of ladyfingers dipped in coffee, layered with a whipped mixture of eggs, sugar, and mascarpone cheese, flavored with cocoa.

Ingredients

  • 6 egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 1 pound mascarpone cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup strong brewed coffee, cooled
  • 24 ladyfingers

Steps

  1. Whisk egg yolks and sugar together in a bowl until thick and pale.
  2. Stir in the milk and mascarpone cheese until smooth.
  3. Whip the heavy cream in a separate bowl until stiff peaks form, then fold into the cheese mixture.
  4. Dip each ladyfinger briefly into the cooled coffee and layer them in the bottom of a dish.
  5. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers, repeat with another layer of coffee-dipped ladyfingers, and top with the remaining mascarpone.
  6. Chill for at least 4 hours, then dust with cocoa powder before serving.
